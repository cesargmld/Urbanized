Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

What we store

  • Without an account: a daily counter of free downloads, keyed by a one-way hash of your IP address and browser; it is not linked to your identity.
  • With an account: your email, credit balance, the IDs of areas you paid for, purchase IDs and amounts, and a sign-in session cookie (90 days).
  • In your browser: language, map position and similar preferences, and a cache of downloaded map data. They never leave your device.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and do not sell data.

Who processes data

  • Cloudflare — hosting and database.
  • Paddle — payments, as Merchant of Record; Paddle collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.
  • Resend — delivery of sign-in emails.
  • Map and data servers your browser requests directly while building a model (OpenStreetMap Overpass mirrors, Esri, Mapterhorn, Overture Maps, Nominatim). They see your IP address as with any website request.
